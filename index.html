వేద పాఠశాల & సేవ

🌅
ఉదయకాల సేవ (Morning Session)

10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

శ్రీ చంద్రమౌళీశ్వర స్పటిక లింగ రుద్రాభిషేకం, నైవేద్యం, మంత్రపుష్పం, స్వామివారి అమృత హస్తాలతో క్షీర తీర్థ ప్రసాద వితరణ మరియు నిత్యాన్నదానం.

🌆
సాయంకాల సేవ (Evening Session)

6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

శ్రీ శారదా దేవి సన్నిధిలో శ్రీచక్ర నవావరణ పూజ, కుంకుమార్చన, మంత్రపుష్పం, బుధవారం లక్ష్మీ నృసింహ స్వామి, శుక్రవారం ఆదిలక్ష్మీ కామేశ్వరీ దేవి విశేష అభిషేకాలు.

The daily activities in the Pushpagiri Sankara Peetham majorly involve Sri Chandramouleeswara Abhishekam in the morning and Sri Chakra Pooja in the evening.

Here, the main deity Sri Chandramouleeswara Swami is worshipped in the form of Spathika Lingam. This Sphatika Lingam is one of the four Shiv Lings brought by Adi Sankaracharya from Himalayas to different Sankara Peethas.

✨ Spathika Lingam in Pushpagiri Sankara Peetham is said to be the biggest of all other Shiv Lings being worshipped in other Sankara Peethams.

Rudra Abhishekam is performed to the Chandramouleeswara Spathika Lingam every day in the morning at Pushpagiri Peetham. One can witness this sacred process everyday in the morning between 10 AM–12 PM. After pooja and Naivedyam, follows uttering of Mantra Pushpam by priests infront of Sri Swamiji, and Sri Swamiji himself will give the Abhishekam Milk as Teertham to all those present at that time.

After distribution of Teertham, Sri Swami's darshan can be done and then follows the Annadaanam open to everyone. There ends the morning session.

దివ్య అభిషేక జల మహిమ (Sacred Healing Water):

Additionally, the Abhisheka Jalam (Water) is also kept at a place, which can be taken by all devotees upon request. It is strongly believed and proven in many cases that consuming this Abhisheka Jalam daily heals any sort of ill health conditions and also works an effective saviour for any problems.

Later in the evening, Shri Chakra Pooja is performed, where another main diety Sri Sarada Devi is worshipped in the form of Sri Chakra through Kumkumaarchana. After Shri Chakra Pooja, Mantra Pushpam is uttered by the priests and then Archana Kumkum is given to all devotees present there.

Peetham also hosts the divine idols of Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Swami and Goddess Sri Aadi Lakshmi Kameshwari. Pooja and Naivaedyam are offered these two dieities also every morning and evening. Special Abhishekam is performed to Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Swamy on every Wednesday evening and to Sri Adi Lakshmi Kameshwari Devi on every Friday evening.

Apart from these regular activities, various other activities like Homams, Kalyanotsavams, Abhishekams, Archana, etc are performed to different dieities occasionally on different auspicious events and days.

History of Pushpagiri (పుష్పగిరి చరిత్ర)

Pushpagiri is situated along the banks of Penna River in Cuddapah (YSR Kadapa) District, Andhra Pradesh. Its sacred roots reach back into Treta Yuga and Vedic antiquity.

సంజీవని పర్వత దివ్య గాథ:

When Lord Hanuman carried the sacred Sanjeevani Parvat to revive Lakshmana during the epic Lanka battle, a holy fragment blessed this earth, sanctifying it as Pushpagiri.

Revered as Pancha Nadi Kshetram (confluence of Penna, Papaghni, Kumudvati, Varkala, and Mandavi), Pushpagiri uniquely hosts Sri Chennakesava Swamy and Sri Santaana Malleshwara Swamy on the same sacred hill, earning the title Dakshina Kashi. Adi Sankaracharya consecrated a powerful Srichakra here, worshipped with deep reverence to this day.

The Rise of Pushpagiri Peetham

For thousands of years, Pushpagiri was a strong spiritual centre known as a prominent Shaiva Peetham in the entire Telugu speaking region. His Holiness Sri Sri Sri Jagadguru Vidyaranya Swami transformed Pushpagiri Shaiva Peetham into Pushpagiri Sankaracharya Peetham.

Having realized the unique spiritual power in the land of Pushpagiri, His Holiness Sri Vidyaranya brought Pushpagiri Shaiva Peetham into the ambit of Jagadguru Sankaracharya Peethams.

This happened as part of Sri Vidyaranya Swami’s spiritual journey when he was the 12th Jagadguru of Sri Sringeri Sharada Peetham during the 14th Century.

For its unique significance as Raja Peetham, Pushpagiri Sankaracharya Peetham was named:

‘శ్రీ శ్రీ శ్రీ జగద్గురు పుష్పగిరి శంకరాచార్య మహా సంస్థానం’

(Sri Sri Sri Jagadguru Pushpagiri Sankaracharya Maha Samsthanam), which is unique unlike the names of other Sankaracharya Peethams.
