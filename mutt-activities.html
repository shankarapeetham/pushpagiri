ఉదయకాల సేవ (Morning Session)

10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

శ్రీ చంద్రమౌళీశ్వర స్పటిక లింగ రుద్రాభిషేకం, నైవేద్యం, మంత్రపుష్పం, స్వామివారి అమృత హస్తాలతో క్షీర తీర్థ ప్రసాద వితరణ, భక్తులకు స్వామివారి దివ్య దర్శనం మరియు నిత్యాన్నదానం.

🌆

సాయంకాల సేవ (Evening Session)

6:00 PM – 9:00 PM

శ్రీ శారదా దేవి సన్నిధిలో శ్రీచక్ర నవావరణ పూజ, కుంకుమార్చన, మంత్రపుష్పం, కుంకుమ ప్రసాద వితరణ. బుధవారం లక్ష్మీ నృసింహ స్వామికి మరియు శుక్రవారం ఆదిలక్ష్మీ కామేశ్వరీ దేవికి విశేష అభిషేకాలు.

The daily activities in the Pushpagiri Sankara Peetham majorly involve Sri Chandramouleeswara Abhishekam in the morning and Sri Chakra Pooja in the evening.

Here, the main deity Sri Chandramouleeswara Swami is worshipped in the form of Spathika Lingam. This Sphatika Lingam is one of the four sacred Shiv Lings brought by Adi Sankaracharya from the sacred Himalayas to the different Sankara Peethams.

✨ The Spathika Lingam in Pushpagiri Sankara Peetham is said to be the biggest of all other Shiv Lings being worshipped across other Sankara Peethams.

Rudra Abhishekam is performed to the Chandramouleeswara Spathika Lingam every day in the morning at Pushpagiri Peetham. Devotees can witness this sacred process everyday between 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM. Following the pooja and Naivedyam, Vedic priests chant sacred Mantra Pushpam in the divine presence of Sri Swamiji, and Sri Swamiji himself distributes the holy Abhishekam Milk as Teertham to all devotees.

After distribution of Teertham, devotees receive Sri Swamiji’s personal darshan and blessings, followed by sacred Nitya Annadaanam open to all devotees and visitors.

దివ్య అభిషేక జల మహిమ (Sacred Healing Water):

Additionally, the Abhisheka Jalam (Water) is preserved at the sanctum, available to all devotees upon request. It is strongly believed and experienced across generations that consuming this Abhisheka Jalam daily provides relief from physical afflictions and acts as a divine saviour for seekers.

Later in the evening, Shri Chakra Pooja is performed in the sanctum of Goddess Sri Sharada Devi through elaborate Kumkumaarchana. Archana Kumkum is distributed to all devotees upon conclusion of the rituals.

The Peetham also enshrines the divine idols of Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Swami and Goddess Sri Aadi Lakshmi Kameshwari. Special Abhishekam is performed to Sri Lakshmi Nrusimha Swamy on every Wednesday evening, and to Sri Adi Lakshmi Kameshwari Devi on every Friday evening.

Apart from regular daily rituals, sacred Homams, Kalyanotsavams, Abhishekams, and special Archanas are conducted on festive occasions throughout the year.
