# Sri Pushpagiri Sankara Peetham (శ్రీ జగద్గురు పుష్పగిరి శంకరాచార్య మహాసంస్థానం)

Official modernized web portal for **Sri Sri Sri Jagadguru Pushpagiri Sankaracharya Maha Samsthanam** — Dakshina Kashi, Avicchinna Sankara Parampara.

## 🌟 Highlights & Features

- **Dynamic Sanctum Theme Engine:** 4 traditional themes (Orange/Kesari, Deep Royal Blue, Sacred Kumkuma Red, Divine Temple Gold) with live interactive switching.
- **Dedicated 48th Peetadhipatulu Section:** Complete biography, sacred photo portraits, and Devanagari/Telugu sloka for H.H. Sri Sri Sri Vidya Sankara Bharati Swamiji.
- **Dedicated Mutt Activities Section:** Sacred daily rituals, Sri Chandramouleeswara Sphatika Lingam Rudra Abhishekam, Sri Chakra Pooja, Abhisheka Jalam healing, and Annadanam.
- **Authentic History of Pushpagiri:** Treta Yuga Sanjeevani Hill history, Pancha Nadi Kshetram, and Sri Chennakesava & Santaana Malleshwara Swamy sannidhis.
- **Annual Sacred Festivals:** Maha Shivaratri, Devi Navaratras, Adi Sankara Jayanti, and Guru Purnima.
- **Pravachanams & Media:** Interactive embedded video discourses and Pravachanam series.
- **Photo Gallery:** Dharma Prachara Yatra galleries with full-screen view.
- **Traditional Sanctum Footer:** 4-column layout with authentic marigold garland trim (`banner-header2.png`).
