His Holiness Sri Sri Sri Vidya Sankara Bharathi Swami is the current and 48th Peethadhipathi of Sri Sri Sri Jagadguru Pushpagiri Sankaracharya Maha Samsthanam.

Sri Vidya Sankara Bharathi Swami follows the lineage as the 48th Peethadhipathi after his Pujya Guru Sri Sri Sri Vidya Nrusimha Bharathi Swami.

Sri Vidya Sankara Bharathi Swami has been successfully carrying forward the legacy of Pushpagiri Peetham in lines of the erstwhile Swamijis in the lineage. In fact, the reputation of Peetham has gone to further extent and is in a good form in the reign of present Swamiji.

His Holiness has been successfully taking forward Sri Adi Sankara's Vedanta Philisophy and its serenity in purifying the life & living. Through Dharma Prachaara Yatras, Sri Swamiji has been extending divine blessings directly and indirectly to the entire Hindu Community in the Telugu-speaking region through active participation in many religious programs like installation of dieties in many new temples, among other activities.

The entire spiritual community has a deep respect for Sri Vidya Sankara Bharathi Swami for his exceptional intellect, knowledge and firm grip on almost all aspects of Sanathan Dharma like Vedas, Shaastras and Upanishads, among others.

Sri Swamiji is reverred for his unique and outstanding excellence in Jyotisha, Vyaakarana, Mantra Sastra, among other crucial areas of Vedic world by many prominent scholars and pandits.

Sri Swamiji has been showing the righteous path to lakhs of devotees, standing as the living form of Jagadguru. Vande Guru Paramparam!

The Rise of Pushpagiri Peetham

For thousands of years, Pushpagiri was a strong spiritual centre known as a prominent Shaiva Peetham. Sri Vidyaranya Swami brought it into the ambit of Jagadguru Sankaracharya Peethams during the 14th Century as Raja Peetham.
